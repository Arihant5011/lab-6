About

Arihant Kumar Mishra, known to readers as Arii, is the author of nine published books and the debut novel The Things We Believe — his first full-length move from poetry into psychological literary fiction.

His work returns often to the same handful of questions: what memory keeps and distorts, how grief reshapes the people left behind, and how identity holds together under the quiet pressure of everyday loneliness. The influence of Kafka and Dostoevsky runs through the writing — less in subject than in method, in a willingness to sit with alienation and unreliable perception rather than resolve them.
